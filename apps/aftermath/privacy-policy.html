Privacy Policy for Aftermath: Money Left to Spend

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short: Aftermath does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no bank connections, and no cloud sync. Everything you enter – your balances, expenses, and pay schedule – stays in a local database on your device, unless and until you choose to export a backup and send it somewhere yourself.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Aftermath: Money Left to Spend mobile app for iOS and Android (the "App"), published by Simon Goodson ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App handles and what happens to it.

Information the App handles

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information. It does not connect to your bank or any other financial service – every figure in it is one you type in yourself. The only data it works with is:

  • Financial figures you enter – your current balance and starting balances, the names, amounts, and due dates of your recurring expenses, any ad hoc expenses you add, and the archived history of past pay periods.
  • Your pay schedule – how often you are paid and on which day or date, so the App can work out each pay period.
  • App preferences – settings such as your currency symbol, default starting balance, light or dark mode, and backup reminder schedule, so the App remembers how you like it configured.

The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. None of the above ever leaves your device automatically. There are no analytics libraries, crash-reporting services, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers built into the App.

Where your data is stored

All of the data described above is stored in a local database on your device only. We do not operate a server, and the App has no network access to any backend of ours – there isn't one.

Backups

The App lets you export a backup of your data on demand. When you do, the backup file is created in the App's private storage on your device, then your device's standard share sheet opens and you choose where that file goes – saved to your device, a cloud storage app you already use, emailed to yourself, or anywhere else your device offers. We never see this file and have no way to access it. Restoring a backup works the same way in reverse: you choose the file, and it's read directly from your device.

Backup files are plain, unencrypted text and contain the financial figures described above, so please keep them somewhere you trust.

Reminders

Optional backup reminders are shown as a prompt inside the App the next time you open it once a backup is due. The App does not send notifications, and no push-notification service or third party is involved.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed, or until you remove it yourself. The App provides tools to:

  • Delete an individual recurring or ad hoc expense, and
  • Fully reset the App, wiping all data (including your history) and returning settings to default.

Uninstalling the App also removes all of its local data from your device. Any backup files you have exported and saved elsewhere are not affected – those are yours to keep or delete.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect information at all in the sense of transmitting or storing it outside your device. It is not directed at children.
