Privacy Policy for Private Miles

Last updated: 19 September 2026

In short: Private Miles does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud sync. Everything the app records — your activities, routes, and notes — stays in a local database on your device, unless and until you choose to export a backup and send it somewhere yourself.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Private Miles mobile app for iOS and Android (the "App"), published by Simon Goodson ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App handles and what happens to it.

Information the App handles

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information. The only data it works with is:

  • Location data — used to record the GPS route, distance, and pace of an exercise while you are actively tracking it, including briefly in the background if your screen locks mid-exercise. Location data is used solely to power this feature and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Exercise data you generate — activity type, duration, distance, named routes, and any short notes you choose to add after finishing an exercise.
  • App preferences — settings such as dark mode, distance units, and reminder schedules, so the App remembers how you like it configured.

None of the above ever leaves your device automatically. There are no analytics libraries, crash-reporting services, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers built into the App.

Where your data is stored

All of the data described above is stored in a local database on your device only. We do not operate a server, and the App has no network access to any backend of ours — there isn't one.

Backups

The App lets you export a backup of your data on demand. When you do, your device's standard share sheet opens and you choose where that file goes — saved to your device, a cloud storage app you already use, emailed to yourself, or anywhere else your device offers. We never see this file and have no way to access it. Restoring a backup works the same way in reverse: you choose the file, and it's read directly from your device.

Notifications

Optional backup-reminder notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using your phone's operating system. No push-notification service or third party is involved, and no data is sent anywhere to make this work.

Voice prompts

If you enable voice prompts, the App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read out your distance, time, and pace during an exercise. This happens entirely on-device — no audio or exercise data is recorded, uploaded, or sent to us or anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed, or until you remove it yourself. The App provides tools to:

  • Delete an individual activity or a saved route,
  • Purge activities older than a date you choose, and
  • Fully reset the App, wiping all data and returning settings to default.

Uninstalling the App also removes all of its local data from your device.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect information at all in the sense of transmitting or storing it outside your device. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date above.
