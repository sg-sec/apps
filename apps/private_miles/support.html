Support & Help

Support guide for using Private Miles.

Private Miles is a simple, private way to track your runs, walks, and rides. Everything below explains what each screen does and how to use it — no technical knowledge needed. If you've never used the app before, start with the Start screen below.

👋Welcome screen

The first time you open Private Miles, you'll see a short welcome screen introducing the app. Tap Get started to move on to the main app. You won't see this screen again unless you reset the app from Settings (more on that below).

🏃Start screen

This is the screen you'll use every time you head out. It's where you set up and begin tracking an exercise. You can always get back here by opening the menu (the three lines in the top-left corner) and choosing Start.

Choosing what you're doing

Use the Exercise type dropdown to choose Running, Walking, Cycling, or any custom type you've added in Settings.

Picking a regular route (optional)

If you've saved routes before, they'll appear under My regular routes. Pick one if you're doing a route you've done before, or leave it set to "No route" if not — you can always name and save the route afterwards instead.

Voice prompts (optional)

Turn this on if you'd like the app to speak your progress out loud while you exercise, so you don't need to check your phone. Once switched on, you can choose to be told your progress:

  • By time — for example, every 5 minutes.
  • By distance — for example, every 1 kilometre or mile.

You can turn either or both on, and adjust how often they announce using the − and + buttons. The app will always speak a final summary when you finish, too.

Countdown before you start (optional)

Turn this on if you'd like a few seconds' warning before tracking begins — handy for putting your phone away first. Choose how many seconds (10 by default). Once you tap Start, a large countdown appears on screen, and you'll hear three beeps in the final three seconds before tracking starts. You can tap Cancel during the countdown if you change your mind.

Starting

Tap the big Start button at the bottom. The first time you do this, your phone will ask permission to use your location — this is needed so the app can record your route and distance. Choose "Allow" (or "Allow While Using App").

While you're exercising

You'll see your elapsed time and distance updating live. Two buttons are available:

  • Pause — temporarily stops the clock and tracking. Useful for traffic lights, water breaks, and so on. Tap Continue to carry on, or Cancel to abandon the exercise entirely without saving it.
  • Stop — ends the exercise and takes you to the finish screen below.

Finishing an exercise

After you tap Stop, you'll see a summary screen where you can:

  • See a map of the route you took (if location was captured), along with your total distance, time, and pace.
  • Expand Splits to see your pace for each individual kilometre or mile (whichever unit you use — see Settings).
  • Choose an existing regular route from the dropdown, or leave it as "No route" and type a name to save this as a new regular route for next time.
  • Add a short note about how it went, if you like.

Finally, tap Save to keep the activity, or Discard if you don't want to keep it (you'll be asked to confirm first).

📖History screen

This screen lists every exercise you've saved, most recent first. Tap the menu and choose History to get there.

Finding a past activity

Use the filters at the top to narrow the list down:

  • Exercise type and Route dropdowns.
  • From / To dates — defaults to the last year.
  • Min / Max distance.

Tap Clear all to remove every filter and see everything again.

Viewing the details of an activity

Tap anywhere on an activity card to expand it. This reveals the route on a map (if one was recorded), any notes you added, and a Splits section you can tap to reveal your per-kilometre or per-mile pace for that activity.

Deleting an activity

Tap the ✕ next to the date on any activity card to delete it. You'll be asked to confirm, since this can't be undone.

📊Statistics screen

This screen shows how you're doing overall. Tap the menu and choose Statistics.

Date range

Use the From / To dates at the top to choose which period the totals below cover (defaults to the last year). Tap Clear all to see stats for all time.

Overall totals

A summary of your total activities, distance, time, and average pace for the selected date range.

By exercise type

The same totals, broken down separately for Running, Walking, Cycling, and any custom types.

Route history

If you've saved any regular routes, pick one from the dropdown here to see a graph of how long each time you've done that route took — a simple way to see whether you're getting faster on your regular routes over time. This graph always shows your entire history on that route, regardless of the date range set above.

⚙️Settings screen

Tap the menu and choose Settings to personalise the app. Each section below has its own ⓘ icon you can tap for a quick reminder of what it does.

Exercise types

Running, Walking, and Cycling are always available. Add your own (e.g. "Hiking" or "Rowing") using the text box and Add button. You can remove any type you've added yourself at any time — your past activities of that type are kept, they just won't offer that type when starting a new exercise unless you add it back.

Regular routes

Pick a saved route from the dropdown and tap Delete route to remove it. Past activities that used that route are kept — they'll just show no route afterwards.

Appearance

Switch between dark and light mode to suit your preference.

Units

Choose whether distances are shown in kilometres or miles throughout the app.

Backup & restore

Export backup saves a copy of all your data to a file, which you can then save anywhere you like — your phone, a cloud storage app, email, wherever you choose. Nobody but you ever sees this file.

Restore backup lets you pick a previously exported file and load it back in. This replaces everything currently in the app, so you'll be asked to confirm first.

Tip: it's worth taking a backup every so often, and definitely before resetting the app (see below).

Backup reminders

Turn this on to get an occasional reminder notification to back up your data, and choose how often (e.g. every 2 weeks).

Purge old activities

Lets you bulk-delete older activities you no longer need — useful for freeing up space. Choose a cut-off date (everything older than that is affected) and, optionally, a single exercise type to limit it to. The screen shows how many activities match before you confirm anything. You'll be offered the chance to back up first, and asked to confirm the exact number of activities that will be deleted before anything happens.

Reset app

Completely wipes the app — every activity, route, and custom exercise type — and returns all settings to their defaults. This cannot be undone, and the app will check whether you've backed up recently before letting you proceed.
