Privacy Policy for Simple Reading Log

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short: Simple Reading Log does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud sync. Everything the App records — the books you've read, your authors, genres, and series, ratings and notes — stays in a local database on your device, unless and until you choose to export a backup and send it somewhere yourself.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Simple Reading Log mobile app for iOS and Android (the "App"), published by Simon Goodson ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App handles and what happens to it.

Information the App handles

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information. The only data it works with is:

  • Book records you enter — title, author, genre, medium (book, e-book, or audio-book), reading status, start and end dates, star rating, the series it belongs to (if any) and its position in that series, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Author, genre, and series lists — built up automatically from the book records you enter, so the App can suggest existing authors, genres, and series as you type. A series also records whether you've marked it completed.
  • App preferences — settings such as dark mode, whether Open Library search is enabled, and your backup-reminder schedule, so the App remembers how you like it configured.

None of the above ever leaves your device automatically. There are no analytics libraries, crash-reporting services, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers built into the App.

Where your data is stored

All of the data described above is stored in a local database on your device only. We do not operate a server, and the App has no network access to any backend of ours — there isn't one.

Open Library search

If you turn on the optional "Open Library search" setting, the App can look up books by title, author, or ISBN using the free, third-party Open Library API (openlibrary.org). This sends only the search terms you type to Open Library, so it can return matching book details for you to add — it never sends any of your existing book records, ratings, or notes. This feature is off by default, only runs when you actively search, and Open Library's own privacy practices apply to that request. See Open Library's privacy policy for details.

Backups

The App lets you export a backup of your data on demand, and export a blank CSV template for bulk-importing books you've already read. When you do, your device's standard share sheet opens and you choose where that file goes — saved to your device, a cloud storage app you already use, emailed to yourself, or anywhere else your device offers. We never see this file and have no way to access it. Restoring a backup or importing a CSV works the same way in reverse: you choose the file, and it's read directly from your device.

Backup reminders

If you turn on backup reminders, the App checks on launch whether a reminder is due and, if so, shows an on-screen prompt asking you to back up. This is a simple in-app check each time you open the App — not a scheduled push notification — so no data is sent anywhere to make it work, and nothing happens while the App is closed.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed, or until you remove it yourself. The App provides tools to:

  • Delete an individual book from its edit screen, and
  • Fully reset the App, wiping all books, authors, genres, series, and settings back to default.

Uninstalling the App also removes all of its local data from your device.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect information at all in the sense of transmitting or storing it outside your device. It is not directed at children.
