Support & Help

Support guide for using Simple Reading Log.

Simple Reading Log is a simple, private way to keep track of the books you've read — no account, no cloud sync. Everything below explains what each screen does and how to use it, no technical knowledge needed. If you've never used the app before, start with the Welcome screen below.

👋Welcome screen

The first time you open Simple Reading Log, you'll see a short welcome screen introducing the app. Tap Next to move on to the main app. You won't see this screen again unless you reset the app from Settings (more on that below).

🏠Home screen

This is the screen you land on every time you open the app. It shows the books you're currently reading (status "In progress"), newest first. You can always get back here by opening the menu (the three lines in the top-left corner) and choosing Home.

  • Tap + Add New Book at the top to log a new book.
  • Tap any book tile to open it on the Edit Book screen.

If you haven't added any books yet, you'll see a short prompt here instead of an empty list.

If a book is part of a series, its series name (and position, if you set one — e.g. "#2") appears under the author on its tile. This is the same wherever book tiles show up: Home, Backlog, History, and a series' own book list on the Series screen.

📚Add New Book screen

Tap the menu and choose Add New Book, or use the button on the Home screen. You'll first be asked how you'd like to add the book:

  • Add Manually — always available. Type in the book's details yourself.
  • Search Open Library — look up a book by title, author, or ISBN and have the title and author filled in for you. This option only appears if you've turned it on in Settings and the app can currently reach Open Library. If it's greyed out, a short note explains why, along with a Retry button to check again.

Filling in the details

Whichever way you start, you'll land on the same form:

  • Title and Author — both required. As you type an author, matching names already in your library appear below the field so you can reuse one instead of retyping it.
  • Series (optional) — if the book's part of a series, start typing its name; matching series you've already added appear below the field, or keep typing to create a new one. You can also give its position in the series (e.g. "2"), shown as "#2" next to the book wherever it appears.
  • Genre — tap to open a tick-box list. Your five most recently used genres appear at the top, followed by the full list.
  • Medium — Book, E-Book, or Audio-Book.
  • Status — To be read, or In progress. (Completed and Abandoned are set later, from the Edit Book screen, once you've actually finished or given up on it.)

Tap Save when you're done, or Cancel to discard it. If a book with the same title and author already exists in your library, you'll be warned before saving a duplicate — useful for catching typos, but you can still save it anyway if it's genuinely a different edition or a re-read.

📋Backlog screen

Lists every book you've added with status "To be read" — the ones waiting in line. Tap the menu and choose Backlog.

Tap Filters to narrow the list by Author, Series, or Medium. Tap any book tile to open it on the Edit Book screen — typically to change its status to "In progress" once you start reading it.

📖History screen

Lists every book you've finished or abandoned. Tap the menu and choose History.

Tap Filters to narrow the list down by:

  • Date range finished — From / To dates.
  • Author.
  • Series.
  • Rating.
  • Completion type — Completed or Abandoned.
  • Medium.

Tap Clear All in the filter sheet to remove every filter and see everything again. Tap any book tile to open it on the Edit Book screen.

🔖Series screen

Lists every series you've tagged a book with. Tap the menu and choose Series.

Each one shows the author, how many books you've added to it, and a Completed switch. This is separate from any individual book's status — it's there so you can mark a whole series finished once the author's actually done writing it, rather than it looking "finished" just because you've read everything currently out. A series stays listed as active, even once you've read every book in it, until you mark it completed yourself — handy for remembering which ones you're still waiting on the next instalment for.

Turn on Hide completed series (shown once you have at least one) to tuck those away. Tap a series' name to see every book in it, in series order, across every status — tap any of those to open it on the Edit Book screen.

Adding a series

Use the text box and Add button at the top of this screen, or just type a new name into the Series field on Add New Book or Edit Book — either way creates it.

✏️Edit Book screen

Opened by tapping any book tile on Home, Backlog, History, or a series' book list. It has the same fields as Add New Book, plus:

  • Status now also offers Completed and Abandoned. Choosing either one automatically records today as the end date.
  • Rating — tap a star to rate the book 0–5.
  • Notes — anything you'd like to remember about it.

Tap Save to keep your changes, or Cancel to discard them. Tap Delete Book at the bottom to remove it permanently — you'll be asked to confirm first, since this can't be undone.

📊Statistics screen

Tap the menu and choose Statistics to see how your reading is going.

Books completed by month

Two grids — this year and last year — each showing how many books you finished in every month.

Genre breakdown

A pie chart of your genres. Use the This Year / Last Year / All-Time toggle above it to change the period it covers.

Favourite author

A ranked list of your authors. This isn't just a simple average rating — an author with a single 5-star book won't automatically outrank one with six books averaging 4.5 stars. The ranking blends each author's average with your overall average across all books, weighted by how many books they have, so it takes more than one high score to top the list.

⚙️Settings screen

Tap the menu and choose Settings to personalise the app. Each section below has its own ⓘ icon you can tap for a quick reminder of what it does.

Open Library API

Turns on the "Search Open Library" option on the Add New Book screen. Only the title or author you type into a search is ever sent to Open Library — nothing else in your library.

Genres

Shows the app's predefined genre list, plus any you've added yourself. Add your own using the text box and Add button. You can remove any genre you've added yourself at any time by tapping the ✕ on its chip — you'll be asked to confirm, and any books currently tagged with it will simply no longer have that genre. The predefined genres can't be removed.

Appearance

Switch between light and dark mode to suit your preference.

Import books

Tap Export Template CSV to get a starter file with the correct columns and date format — including optional Series and Series Position columns — fill it in with books you've already read, then tap Import from CSV to bring them in. The app checks the file's format before importing anything.

Tap Export All Books to save your whole library as a CSV in that same format — handy for keeping an external copy, or bulk-editing it and importing it back in.

Backup & restore

Shows when you last backed up. Export backup saves a copy of all your books, authors, genres, and settings to a file, which you can then save anywhere you like — your phone, a cloud storage app, email, wherever you choose. Nobody but you ever sees this file.

Restore backup lets you pick a previously exported file and load it back in. This replaces everything currently in the app, so you'll be asked to confirm first.

Tip: it's worth taking a backup every so often, and definitely before resetting the app (see below).

Backup reminders

Turn this on to get an occasional on-screen reminder to back up your data when you open the app, and choose how often — weekly, monthly, or annually.

Reset app

Permanently erases every book, author, genre, and setting, returning the app to how it was on first install. This cannot be undone, and you'll be offered the chance to back up first before it happens.
